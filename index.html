Spot the Hazard.

Our proposed organic campaign turns a common warehouse-safety scenario into a simple interactive challenge that audiences can try without paid advertising.

A proposed awareness campaign.

“Spot the Hazard” is a zero-budget marketing concept from our A6 strategy. A warehouse image or short scenario would contain several potential hazards, with the audience invited to identify them before the answers are revealed.

This is a marketing campaign concept, not a feature of the current SafeLearn application prototype.
