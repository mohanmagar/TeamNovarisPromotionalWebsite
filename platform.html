Why SafeLearn

Move beyond passive safety training.

SafeLearn combines short learning content, interactive activities, hazard identification, quizzes and progress tracking in a single browser-based experience.

1
Hazard Perception in the Warehouse

Focuses attention on recognising risks in warehouse environments.

2
Manual Handling & Ergonomic Safety

Builds awareness around safer manual-handling decisions.

3
Unsafe Acts & Near-Miss Reporting

Connects learning with reporting and safety awareness.

4
Working at Height

Addresses racking and mezzanine safety scenarios.

Platform features

Designed around active participation.

The prototype brings training, challenge, feedback and monitoring into one experience.

01

Interactive modules

Structured content supports focused learning across four core safety topics.

02

Quizzes

Knowledge checks use an 80% pass threshold and help users measure understanding.

03

Games & challenges

Hazard Hunt, Safe-or-Unsafe and scenario activities make safety learning more engaging.

04

Progress tracking

Safety XP, completion status and a leaderboard give users visible feedback on progress.

05

Incident reporting

Users can record incidents and near misses through a guided reporting workflow.

06

Admin monitoring

Administrators can review employee completion information and reported incidents.
