Campaign concept

Spot the Hazard.

A simple, interactive safety challenge designed for organic social media and website engagement. Can you identify all five warehouse hazards?

Campaign concept

Spot the Hazard is a marketing activity.

The A6 strategy proposes an image or short warehouse scenario containing several potential safety hazards. Audiences are invited to identify the hazards, then the correct answers and short explanations are revealed.

✓
Zero-budget

Uses organic channels rather than paid advertising.

✓
Easy to share

Can be adapted for LinkedIn, social media posts, screenshots or short demonstrations.

✓
Separate from the app

This page presents the campaign idea only. It is not being claimed as a current SafeLearn application feature.
